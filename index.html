About Luminarc Works

Visual Artistry & Digital Crafts

Luminarc Works is a creative visual practice built on the passion for freelance photography, event coverage, and multimedia storytelling.

Whether shooting on-site event highlights, community festivals (such as SINDAK GTEX Spookfest), portrait sessions, or post-processing footage, Luminarc Works brings a sharp eye for lighting, detail, and emotion.

Beyond photography, the studio provides end-to-end creative solutions—including cinematic video editing, custom graphic branding, and responsive web development to showcase your vision to the world.
